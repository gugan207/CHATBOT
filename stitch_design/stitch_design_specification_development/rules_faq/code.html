You can renew standard items up to two times, provided no other patron has placed a hold on them. Renewals can be processed online through the 'My Loans' portal or in person at the circulation desk.

Lost items must be reported immediately. You will be responsible for the replacement cost of the item plus a non-refundable $15 processing fee. If the item is found within 30 days of payment, a partial refund may be issued.
